**What I'll tell you for free:**

I measure what I claim. Not *"it feels synced"* — the phase error, in beats.
Not *"onsets detected"* — 5.28 a second against the 1.07 actually in the track.
A test proving the output is finite and in range will pass happily while the
screen renders solid black. That has caught me four separate times. Now I
render it and **look**.

Zero dependencies, where it matters. FFTs, parsers, servers, colour maths —
hand-written, because a binary under a megabyte that runs on a Pi Zero beats
the afternoon a library would have saved.

Finished means documented. A spec, a licence, CI, and a demo a stranger can
run in under a minute. Otherwise it isn't done, it's working on my machine.

I'd rather be corrected than agreed with. The last thing I was confident and
wrong about cost me a week and taught me more than the month before it.

**What I won't:** what I'm building, what it's called, or when.
